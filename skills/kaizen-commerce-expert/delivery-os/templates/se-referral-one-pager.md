# Shopify SE Referral Brief

Internal template for Shopify Solutions Engineers, AEs, and partner managers. Keep this

## When to refer KaizenCommerce

Refer KaizenCommerce when a merchant is moving to Shopify POS and the launch risk is operational,
not just technical setup. One strong referral path is a merchant with a capable internal technical
team that wants to implement themselves, but needs a paid diagnostic and advisory engagement. The
Blueprint Diagnostic is KaizenCommerce's paid pre-implementation audit and launch plan, built to
avoid a weak launch plan, missed workflow gaps, or a risky store rollout.

KaizenCommerce also still handles full implementation. That is the stronger path when the merchant
needs Shopify POS delivery, operational coverage, existing tech stack integrations, and workflows
built around Shopify POS: Special Orders-style requests, robust inventory management,
customizable Purchase Order flows, deposits, vendor ordering, customer follow-up, or store-team
exception handling.

Strong fit signals:

- Two or more locations, with retail staff using POS daily
- Legacy POS migration pressure from contract renewal, poor reporting, or unreliable inventory
- Owner / COO is worried about launch disruption, inventory shrink, or store-team readiness
- Gift card, loyalty, accounting, ERP, WMS, wholesale, or existing-stack integration complexity
- Need for Lightspeed-style Special Orders functionality, deposits, vendor ordering, or customer
  request workflows that Shopify POS does not recreate natively out of the box
- Need for robust inventory management, customizable Purchase Order flows, or store-team workflow
  coverage beyond a standard POS setup
- Need for a controlled cutover, not a simple POS configuration
- Strong technical team wants outside Shopify POS architecture, QA, and launch sequencing guidance
  without handing over the full implementation
- Merchant wants KaizenCommerce to own full implementation for Shopify POS plus existing-stack
  integrations and custom operational workflows around Special Orders, inventory exceptions, or
  store-team follow-up

Weak fit signals:

- Ecommerce-only implementation with no POS, store operations, or launch workflow complexity
- Single-location setup with clean catalog and no operational complexity
- Merchant only wants hardware setup or theme work
- No owner / COO access
- No realistic launch driver

## What KaizenCommerce does

KaizenCommerce helps retailers move to Shopify POS without treating launch as a spreadsheet import
or a basic configuration checklist. For merchants with a strong internal implementation team, the
work can stop at Blueprint Diagnostic + Advisory: the paid pre-implementation audit and launch plan.
KaizenCommerce maps the operating risks, reviews data and workflow readiness, clarifies launch
decisions, and gives the internal team a practical plan they can execute.

If the merchant wants KaizenCommerce to own delivery, the same Blueprint can expand into full
implementation: Shopify POS setup, data/workflow preparation, store-team testing, pilot launch,
phased rollout, launch support, existing-stack integrations, and custom operational workflows for
cases like Special Orders, robust inventory management, customizable Purchase Order flows, and
store-team workflow.

Buyer-facing promise:

> We map the risk before implementation, validate the data before cutover, and avoid asking stores
> to learn the new workflow for the first time on launch day.
