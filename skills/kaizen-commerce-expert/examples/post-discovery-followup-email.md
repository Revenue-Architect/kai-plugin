# Good vs Bad: Post Discovery Follow-Up Email

Use when drafting a post-discovery or post-Blueprint follow-up.

## Bad

"Hi, thanks for your time today. As discussed, our team can help streamline your operations and
build a robust Shopify solution. Please don't hesitate to reach out with any questions."

## Why It Fails

- Uses banned phrases
- Says nothing specific from the call
- Sounds like a template
- No clear next action

## Good

"Hi Celi,

The main thing I took from today's call is that the POS decision is really an inventory trust
decision. Your team can sell through the current setup, but they do not fully trust the inventory
picture between online and in-store, so staff keep absorbing the cleanup manually.

The right next step is a Blueprint. We would use that to confirm the migration scope, review the
data export quality, map the Shopify POS setup, and identify whether AnyDB belongs in a later
operations phase or should stay out of scope.

I can send the Blueprint outline next if you want to review the steps before we schedule it."

## Why It Works

- First line is about the merchant, not Kaizen
- Preserves the real diagnosis
- Keeps AnyDB as conditional
- Ends with one clear action
