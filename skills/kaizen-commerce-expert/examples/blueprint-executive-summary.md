# Good vs Bad: Blueprint Executive Summary

Use when writing or reviewing the executive summary of a Blueprint report.

## Bad

"KaizenCommerce completed a comprehensive assessment of the client's operations and identified
several opportunities for improvement across systems, data, and workflows. The current setup is
creating inefficiencies, and a more robust solution is recommended to support growth."

## Why It Fails

- Could describe any merchant
- No system names
- No quantified operational pain
- No clear recommendation
- Uses weak consultant language instead of operator language

## Good

"Boardrider Supply's five stores are running daily operations through Lightspeed, a shared
spreadsheet, and manual end-of-day reconciliation. The setup works well enough to keep stores
selling, but it breaks down where inventory has to stay accurate across stores and ecommerce.
The team is spending roughly 12.5 hours per week reconciling inventory, and online oversells are
still happening because the store-level inventory picture is not trusted.

The right next step is a Shopify POS migration with parallel validation before cutover. AnyDB
should stay out of Phase 1 unless purchasing and vendor workflows are confirmed as separate
scope. The migration should focus first on products, customers, opening inventory by location,
staff permissions, hardware readiness, and a training plan that store managers can run without
Kaizen in the room."

## Why It Works

- Names the current systems
- Balances what works with what breaks
- Connects pain to the recommended path
- Keeps AnyDB restrained
- Gives the buyer a concrete next step
