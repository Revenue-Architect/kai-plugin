Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Kaizen Commerce operates the Kaizen Commerce ChatGPT plugin and its hosted MCP service. This policy explains what the service processes when you connect it to ChatGPT and KaizenOS.

Information processed

  • OAuth account information, such as your authenticated user identifier, email address, and basic profile claims supplied by Supabase.
  • Your ChatGPT requests, tool inputs, and tool results when you invoke the plugin.
  • KaizenOS CRM and project records that you specifically ask the plugin to read, preview, or change and that your KaizenOS role permits it to access.
  • Operational request metadata and security logs handled by the hosting and authentication providers.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce permissions, retrieve requested KaizenOS context, return analysis or drafts, protect the service, and maintain an audit trail for approved KaizenOS changes. The plugin does not use this information for advertising or sell it.

Service providers and sharing

Requests may be processed by OpenAI/ChatGPT, Supabase for authentication and KaizenOS data services, Netlify for hosting and request execution, and KaizenOS as the connected source of truth. The plugin does not share connected records with other users unless your organization’s own systems or sharing settings allow that access.

Retention

KaizenOS records remain subject to the retention rules of the connected KaizenOS account or organization. The plugin does not intentionally create a separate database of CRM or project records. Transient processing and provider operational logs may be retained for the period reasonably needed for service operation, security, troubleshooting, and legal obligations, subject to the applicable provider policies.

Your choices

You can stop using or disconnect the plugin, revoke its OAuth authorization, and request help through GitHub support issues. Your KaizenOS administrator controls your underlying account, role, and record permissions.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or its processing changes. The current version will remain available at this URL.
